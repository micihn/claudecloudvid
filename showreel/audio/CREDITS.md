# Sound credits

Every sound in the score is a real recording released under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**.
Nothing is synthesized. The only processing is repitching, trimming, and a convolution hall reverb.

| Source | License | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE) (Versilian Studios) | CC0 1.0 | Violin, viola and cello sections (sustain, spiccato, pizzicato), contrabass pizzicato, harp, French horn, tenor trombone, tuba, flute, glockenspiel and its glissandi, marimba, timpani and rolls, bass drum, crash/suspended cymbals, gong, snare roll, triangle, claves, wood clicks, glass breaking |
| [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) | CC0 1.0 | Rubbed wine glasses, hand chimes, tubular bells, hand claps |
| [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) — UI Audio, Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds, Casino Audio (via [lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds](https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds)) | CC0 1.0 | Switch clicks (the hi-hats), glass taps, clay chips and dice (the "marbles") |
| Ben Burnes packs — Keyboard Sounds, Pill Bottles, Rubik's Cube, Bottle Plops (same collection) | CC0 1.0 | Typing, the Model D switch (final click), pill bottle rolls, cube twists, plops |
| "100 CC0 SFX" packs (same collection) | CC0 1.0 | Key jingles (the shaker), pebbles |

I couldn't find a CC0 recording of actual marbles. Clay chips, dice and pebbles stand in for them.
